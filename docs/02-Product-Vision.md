# PCEA Connect – Product Vision
**Version 1.0 | July 2026**

## Our Mission
To be the digital operating system of the Presbyterian Church of East Africa, connecting every member, ministry, and leader.

## Product Pillars
1. Member Engagement – daily Bible, feed, events, prayer.
2. Church Administration – membership, attendance, committees.
3. Financial Stewardship – M-Pesa giving, statements, budgets.
4. Communication – announcements, SMS, WhatsApp, USSD.
5. Pastoral Care – prayer requests, visits, tasks.
6. Ministry Collaboration – each ministry has its own hub.
7. Leadership Intelligence – drill-down analytics, CSV/PDF exports.

## Success Metrics (Pilot)
- Weekly active users > 50%
- Giving transactions > 10 per week
- Event registrations > 5 per week
- Prayer requests > 5 per week
- Feed posts > 20 per week
