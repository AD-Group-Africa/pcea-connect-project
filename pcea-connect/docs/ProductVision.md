# Product Vision

## Purpose
PCEA Connect aims to provide a unified digital platform for the Presbyterian Church of East Africa, enabling seamless communication, administration, and engagement for its members and leadership.

## Users
- **Members**: Daily Bible reading, giving, events, prayer requests.
- **Ministers & Elders**: Pastoral care, attendance, member management.
- **Treasurers**: Financial contributions, M-Pesa reconciliation, reports.
- **Youth Leaders**: Youth fellowship feed, events, mentorship.
- **Church Administrators**: Church hierarchy, roles, audit logs.
- **National Leadership**: Real-time dashboards and exports.

## Goals
- Connect 4+ million members across Kenya, Tanzania, and beyond.
- Digitize church administration and member engagement.
- Provide real-time analytics and reporting for leadership.

## Non-goals
- Replace in-person fellowship and worship.
- Serve as a general-purpose social media platform.

## Success Metrics
- Adoption rate among congregations.
- User engagement metrics (e.g., daily active users).
- Financial contributions processed through the platform.
