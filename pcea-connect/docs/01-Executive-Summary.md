# PCEA Connect – Executive Summary
**Version 1.0 | July 2026 | Afrika Digitalis**

## What is PCEA Connect?
PCEA Connect is the official digital operating system for the Presbyterian Church of East Africa. It combines church administration, daily member engagement, and communication tools into a single, modern platform.

## Why Was It Built?
The PCEA needed a digital backbone to connect its 4+ million members across Kenya, Tanzania, and beyond. Existing solutions were fragmented, desktop-only, or failed to capture the church's unique hierarchy and ministry structure.

## What Problem Does It Solve?
- Disconnected congregations ? real-time communication and a shared church directory.
- Manual administration ? digital membership, attendance, events, and reports.
- Limited giving options ? M-Pesa integration, statements, and donor history.
- Youth disengagement ? social feed, Bible streaks, livestream, and ministry communities.
- No leadership visibility ? drill-down analytics from National to Congregation level.

## Who Uses It?
- Members – daily Bible reading, giving, events, prayer requests.
- Ministers & Elders – pastoral care, attendance, member management.
- Treasurers – financial contributions, M-Pesa reconciliation, reports.
- Youth Leaders – youth fellowship feed, events, mentorship.
- Church Administrators – church hierarchy, roles, audit logs.
- National Leadership – real-time dashboards and exports.

## Deployment
Pilot: Single congregation on a cloud server (Hetzner CX11). Scale: Docker Compose with PostgreSQL, Redis, Nginx.
